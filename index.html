Who we are

Energy is where law, markets, and the planet meet.

The Energy Law Society exists to give Lincoln Alexander law students practical exposure to the field: how energy projects get built, financed, regulated, and fought over. From power purchase agreements to pipeline disputes, energy work sits at the centre of the modern economy.

We launched in 2026, and we are building the club from the ground up: connecting students with the lawyers doing this work in Toronto and around the world, and developing the skills to join them.

What we're planning

Four programs. One goal.

We are a brand-new club, so everything below is what we plan to build in our first year. Join us and help shape it.

01

Speaker Panels

Panels and talks that will feature the best energy lawyers in the world, from Toronto and far beyond: regulation, projects, deals, and disputes.

02

Networking Sessions

Planned sessions where students meet energy practitioners, in-house counsel, and alumni for mentorship and candid career insight.

03

Energy Arbitration Competition

Our planned flagship event: an annual skills-based competition on an energy dispute, argued before practising lawyers.

04

Research & Thought Pieces

We want to involve law students directly in energy law research and student-written thought pieces on the issues shaping the field.
